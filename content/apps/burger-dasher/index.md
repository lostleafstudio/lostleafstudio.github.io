Stack burgers, dodge danger, and dash through the kitchen in this fast-paced retro arcade game!

## Classic Arcade Action

Step into the shoes of a chef racing against time and mischievous food enemies. Drop burger ingredients through platforms, stack them perfectly, and complete delicious orders before the kitchen gets too hot to handle!

## Easy to Learn, Challenging to Master

Simple controls meet devious level design. Walk across burger parts to drop them down, trap enemies between buns, and use strategy to survive increasingly chaotic kitchens. Every level brings new challenges and tighter timing.

## Features

- Authentic retro arcade gameplay with modern polish
- Dozens of handcrafted levels with increasing difficulty
- Colorful pixel art graphics
- Tight, responsive platformer controls
- Chase high scores and perfect completions
- No ads interrupting your gameplay
- Works offline - play anywhere, anytime

## Perfect for

- Fans of classic arcade games
- Quick gaming sessions on the go
- Players who love skill-based challenges
- Anyone nostalgic for the golden age of arcades

Built with love by indie developers who grew up in arcades. **Burger Dasher** captures that pure, addictive gameplay that kept us pumping quarters into machines.
